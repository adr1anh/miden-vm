Typed environment commitments compatible with the VM's native EnvNode and Namespace types.<br />Constructors record raw hash payloads in the advice map; fixed-size openings authenticate<br />those payloads. Namespace derivation alone does not authenticate a binding or authorize a root.<br />The library module constructs complete flat procedure trees; paths verifies and updates<br />individual bindings through typed binary ancestors.<br /><br />Cycle counts are raw VM operations on successful execution, including inlined helpers,<br />local-frame setup/cleanup, and Noops for skipped loops or empty branches. They exclude<br />invocation boundaries, control/basic-block rows, operation packing/padding, host advice<br />preparation, and chiplet work.<br />
